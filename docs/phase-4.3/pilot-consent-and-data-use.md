# RasoiIQ — Pilot Participation Consent & Data Use Notice

**Project:** RasoiIQ Household Validation Pilot  
**Version:** 1.0 (Phase 4.3)  
**Applicability:** Supervised In-Home & LAN-Connected Household Pilot Program  
**Cost to Participant:** ₹0 / Free of Charge  

---

## 1. Introduction & Welcome

Thank you for agreeing to participate in the RasoiIQ Supervised Household Pilot. RasoiIQ is an intelligent, household-centric meal planning and smart grocery management assistant built specifically for Indian homes.

Before we begin, we want you to understand exactly how this pilot works, what information is recorded, how it is protected, and your rights as a participant. We prioritize privacy, simplicity, and safety.

---

## 2. What Information We Collect

To personalize your weekly meal plans, calculate accurate nutritional balance, and help coordinate with your domestic cook, RasoiIQ collects:

1. **Household Profile**:
   - A friendly household display name (e.g., "The Sharma Family").
   - An email address used solely for local account identification.
2. **Family Member Details**:
   - First names or nicknames of participating family members.
   - Age categories (Adult / Child) to estimate caloric requirements.
   - Dietary preferences (e.g., Vegetarian, Jain, Vegan, Eggetarian, Non-Vegetarian).
   - Declared medical food allergies (e.g., Peanuts, Tree Nuts, Gluten, Dairy, Mustard).
3. **Pantry Inventory**:
   - Food staples, flours, oils, and spices you currently have in your kitchen.
4. **App Usage & Feedback**:
   - Recipe ratings, dish swaps, meal plan approvals, and interview notes regarding time saved and ease of use.

---

## 3. What Information We NEVER Collect

We strictly respect your boundaries. RasoiIQ does **NOT** collect, request, or store:
- **No Financial Data**: No credit/debit card numbers, bank details, UPI IDs, or wallets. All shopping carts and checkouts in this pilot are 100% simulated.
- **No Government IDs**: No Aadhaar, PAN, passport, or voter ID details.
- **No Precise GPS Tracking**: We do not access your device's live geolocation.
- **No Domestic Cook Personal Data**: We do not collect your cook’s phone number, bank details, or private information. The cook briefing is formatted for you to share directly on your own WhatsApp.
- **No Unconsented Audio/Video**: We do not record background conversations in your home.

---

## 4. How Your Data is Used & Protected

- **Local Storage Discipline**: All profile and pantry data is stored securely in an isolated, encrypted local database on the founder's workstation during the pilot. It is never exposed to public internet scrapers.
- **AI Privacy Guardrails**: When generating meal suggestions via Google Gemini AI, only anonymized dietary constraints (e.g., "Family of 4, North Indian, Vegetarian, Peanut-Free") are transmitted. Your names, emails, and address are stripped prior to AI generation.
- **Zero Third-Party Commercial Sale**: Your information is never sold, leased, or shared with third-party advertisers, data aggregators, or marketing firms.
- **No Real Transactions**: Quick-commerce prices and delivery estimations (Blinkit, Zepto, Instamart, BigBasket) are simulated benchmarks used for user experience testing only.

---

## 5. Voluntary Participation & Right to Deletion

Your participation in this pilot is completely voluntary.
- **Withdraw Anytime**: You may pause or end your participation at any point during the 7-day period without giving a reason.
- **Right to Complete Erasure**: At the end of the pilot (or immediately upon request), you can ask the founder to permanently delete your household record, pantry inventory, and meal plans from the database. A verification command (`DELETE FROM "Household" WHERE id = ...`) will purge all associated records within 24 hours.
