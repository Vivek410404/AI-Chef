## 2. Deep-Dive Audit: Expanded Gujarati & Kathiyawadi Dishes

### 1. Kathiyawadi Ringna No Olo (Baingan Bharta)
- **Recipe ID**: `recipe-ringna-no-olo`
- **Cuisine**: Kathiyawadi
- **Servings**: 4
- **Authentic Composition**:
  - Purple Roasting Baingan: 600g (large round purple brinjal).
  - Tomatoes: 200g finely chopped.
  - Green garlic / fresh garlic: 20g crushed.
  - Green chillies: 20g finely minced.
  - Mustard oil: 35ml.
  - Spices: Turmeric (5g), red chilli powder (10g), coriander powder (10g), salt (10g).
- **Culinary Authenticity**: Mandates direct open-flame roasting until charred and blistered, yielding the essential rustic smoky aroma before mashing and tempering.
- **Nutrition (IFCT 2017)**: 165 kcal, 3.8g protein, 18.2g carbs, 8.5g fat, 6.2g fiber.
- **Dietary Suitability**: Pure Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free. Jain modification notes document replacing brinjal with green plantain/bottle gourd mash.

### 2. Authentic Surati Undhiyu with Methi Muthiya
- **Recipe ID**: `recipe-surati-undhiyu`
- **Cuisine**: Gujarati
- **Servings**: 4
- **Authentic Composition**:
  - Surti Flat Beans (Papdi): 250g stringed.
  - Fresh Green Tuver Lilva: 150g.
  - Purple Yam (Kand): 150g cubed.
  - Baby Potatoes: 150g cubed.
  - Baby Eggplants: 150g slit crosswise.
  - Fenugreek (Methi) leaves: 100g fresh.
  - Coarse Besan: 80g for muthiya binding.
  - White Sesame seeds: 20g.
  - Groundnut oil: 50ml.
- **Culinary Authenticity**: Incorporates spiced fried fenugreek dumplings (muthiya) steamed alongside winter root vegetables and legumes in a green coconut-sesame masala paste.
- **Nutrition (IFCT 2017)**: 385 kcal, 11.2g protein, 48.6g carbs, 16.5g fat, 10.5g fiber.
- **Dietary Suitability**: Pure Vegetarian. (Non-Jain due to root vegetables; Jain adaptation replaces purple yam/potatoes with raw plantain).

### 3. Traditional Sweet & Sour Gujarati Kadhi
- **Recipe ID**: `recipe-gujarati-kadhi`
- **Cuisine**: Gujarati
- **Servings**: 4
- **Authentic Composition**:
  - Sour Curd / Dahi: 200g whisked.
  - Besan (Gram flour): 30g.
  - Jaggery (Gor): 25g.
  - Ginger-green chilli paste: 15g.
  - Ghee tempering (Vaghar): 20g ghee, mustard seeds (5g), cumin (5g), fenugreek seeds (methi dana 3g), cloves (2g), cinnamon (2g), curry leaves (1 sprig).
- **Culinary Authenticity**: Thin, velvety sweet-and-sour profile characteristic of Surat and Ahmedabad homestyle meals, without turmeric (pale cream appearance).
- **Nutrition (IFCT 2017)**: 145 kcal, 5.2g protein, 14.8g carbs, 7.2g fat, 1.2g fiber.
- **Dietary Suitability**: Pure Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Jain Friendly (when ginger omitted).

### 4. Baked Gujarati Handvo (Savory Vegetable Cake)
- **Recipe ID**: `recipe-gujarati-handvo`
- **Cuisine**: Gujarati
- **Servings**: 4
- **Authentic Composition**:
  - Coarse Rice: 200g.
  - Chana Dal: 80g split Bengal gram.
  - Toor Dal: 40g pigeon pea.
  - Urad Dal: 30g black gram split.
  - Sour Curd: 100g for fermentation.
  - Grated bottle gourd (Doodhi): 100g.
  - White Sesame Seeds: 20g for crust.
  - Mustard seeds (6g), green chillies (10g), ajwain (4g), oil (30ml).
- **Culinary Authenticity**: Overnight fermented multi-lentil batter poured into a skillet or tin, topped with sesame-mustard vaghar, baked to a crisp golden crust and moist crumb.
- **Nutrition (IFCT 2017)**: 260 kcal, 9.5g protein, 36.8g carbs, 8.5g fat, 4.8g fiber.
- **Dietary Suitability**: Pure Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Jain Friendly.

### 5. Crispy Methi Na Gota (Dakor Style)
- **Recipe ID**: `recipe-methi-na-gota`
- **Cuisine**: Gujarati
- **Servings**: 4
- **Authentic Composition**:
  - Coarse Besan: 150g.
  - Fresh Methi (Fenugreek) Leaves: 100g finely chopped.
  - Coarsely crushed black peppercorns: 8g.
  - Ajwain: 4g.
  - Baking soda: 2g.
  - Sugar: 10g.
  - Oil for deep frying: 60ml absorbed.
- **Culinary Authenticity**: Puffy, golden Dakor-style fritters with sweet-bitter balance and coarse black pepper crunch, served alongside kadhi or fried green chillies.
- **Nutrition (IFCT 2017)**: 230 kcal, 7.8g protein, 24.5g carbs, 11.2g fat, 4.2g fiber.
- **Dietary Suitability**: Pure Vegetarian, Jain Friendly, Vegan.

### 6. Traditional Kathiyawadi Bajra No Rotlo
- **Recipe ID**: `recipe-bajra-no-rotlo`
- **Cuisine**: Kathiyawadi
- **Servings**: 4
- **Authentic Composition**:
  - Pearl Millet Flour (Bajri no lot): 200g freshly milled.
  - Warm water: 120ml.
  - Desi Ghee / Makhan: 25g.
  - Salt: 5g.
- **Culinary Authenticity**: Traditional hand-flattened Kathiyawadi technique without rolling pin, roasted on a clay tava (*lodhi*) over wood or flame until puffed.
- **Nutrition (IFCT 2017)**: 195 kcal, 5.8g protein, 34.2g carbs, 4.1g fat, 6.5g fiber.
- **Dietary Suitability**: 100% Pure Vegetarian, Jain Friendly, Vegan (when oil used instead of ghee), Gluten-Free.
