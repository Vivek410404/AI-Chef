# RasoiIQ Phase 4: Authentic Gujarati & Kathiyawadi Recipe Catalog

## 1. Regional Culinary Rationale

For the Western India pilot (Ahmedabad, Vadodara, Surat, Rajkot, Mumbai), culinary preferences diverge significantly from pan-Indian restaurant standards. Home cooking centers on two distinct yet complementary regional traditions:
1. **Gujarati Cuisine (Central & South Gujarat / Surat)**: Characterized by delicate sweet-sour balancing (*khattu-meethu*), jaggery, lemon juice, whole spices, steamed snack delicacies (*farsan*), and festival staples like Undhiyu.
2. **Kathiyawadi Cuisine (Saurashtra)**: Rustic, robust, and hearty cooking developed for dry rural landscapes. Emphasizes stone-ground pearl millet (*Bajra no Rotlo*), wood-roasted eggplants (*Ringna no Olo*), quick tomato gravies topped with crisp sev (*Sev Tameta*), and raw onions, jaggery chunks, and garlic chutneys.

In Phase 4, the RasoiIQ catalog expanded from 23 to **32 authentic recipes**, registering **10 new verified regional ingredients** backed by ICMR-NIN IFCT 2017 nutritional profiles.
