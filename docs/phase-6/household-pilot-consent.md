## 1. Welcome to the RasoiIQ Pilot

RasoiIQ is an experimental, intelligent meal planning and household grocery operating system designed specifically for Indian families. 

Before participating in this supervised pilot, please read this plain-English information document carefully. We want you to fully understand what RasoiIQ does, how it works, what data is collected, and what your responsibilities are.

---

## 2. Key Things You Must Know

### 1. This is an Experimental Pilot
RasoiIQ is early-stage software undergoing active evaluation. It is hosted locally on the supervisor's workstation and accessed over your home Wi-Fi network. Features may be updated during the pilot.

### 2. AI Suggestions May Contain Errors
Meal plans generated by the AI Chef are automated culinary recommendations. You and your family retain complete authority to review, modify, or reject any suggested dish.

### 3. Nutrition is an Estimate, Not Medical Advice
Nutritional information (Calories, Protein, Carbohydrates, Fat) is calculated from standard raw ingredient composition data published by the ICMR - National Institute of Nutrition (IFCT 2017). Actual nutritional values vary based on cooking technique, brand, and oil absorption. It is not medical or clinical nutrition guidance.

### 4. Allergy Warnings are NOT a Guarantee
RasoiIQ filters recipes against common allergen tags. However, **RasoiIQ does NOT guarantee allergen-free food**. Household members with severe food allergies must independently inspect commercial packaging ingredient labels and take precautions against domestic kitchen cross-contact.

### 5. Retailer Prices are Sample Data
Product listings, prices, and stock indicators for Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart, and BigBasket are **illustrative sample data** for budgeting practice. They are not live market quotes and do not represent partnerships.

### 6. Checkout is 100% Simulated (Zero Money Charged)
When you click "Confirm Order" in the grocery basket, **no real payment gateway is invoked**. You will never be asked for your debit card, credit card, UPI PIN, or net-banking credentials. No money is deducted.

### 7. Physical Concierge Purchases are Completely Separate
If you choose to have the founder purchase real groceries on your behalf during the pilot, this is arranged entirely offline via mutual agreement. Reimbursing the founder occurs outside the software.

## 3. What Information We Collect & How It Is Used

| Information | Purpose | Who Can Access It |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Account Data** (Name, Email, Phone) | Account login, email verification, session recovery | You and authorized platform administrators |
| **Family Member Names & Age Groups** | Scaling recipe portion sizes for meals | Members of your household |
| **Dietary Preferences & Dislikes** | Tailoring recipes to Jain, Veg, or regional styles | Members of your household and AI generator |
| **Allergy Declarations** | Highlighting alerts in the Maharaj cook digest | Household members and your domestic cook |
| **Pantry Inventory** | Preventing duplicate grocery purchases | Members of your household |
| **Daily Meal Plans** | Coordinating food preparation | Household members and domestic cook |

### Use of External AI Services
To generate creative meal plans, anonymized recipe prompts (such as *"Family of 4, Vegetarian, Gujarati, avoiding peanuts"*) are transmitted to Google Gemini Flash-Lite. **No personal names, email addresses, phone numbers, or passwords are ever sent to external AI providers.**

## 4. Your Rights as a Pilot Participant

- **Right to Withdraw:** You may pause or permanently withdraw from the pilot at any time, for any reason, with zero penalty.
- **Right to Data Access & Correction:** You may view, correct, or update your household profile at any time through the application interface.
- **Right to Data Deletion:** Upon request or pilot conclusion, all your household data, member records, and pantry items will be permanently erased from the PostgreSQL database within 24 hours. Immutable security audit logs retaining anonymized actor IDs are maintained for safety verification.
