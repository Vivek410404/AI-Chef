# RasoiIQ Phase 6.0 — Maharaj Cook Workflow & Data Minimization Audit

**Milestone:** Phase 6.0 Controlled Household Pilot Readiness  
**Target:** 3 Supervised Pilot Households with Domestic Cooks (Maharaj)  
**Core Principles:** Culinary Clarity, Allergy Highlighting, and Strict Data Minimization  

---

## 1. Executive Summary

The Maharaj cook workflow is designed specifically for domestic cooks, housekeepers, and family meal preparers. It provides an intuitive, high-clarity daily culinary preparation digest. Crucially, the system enforces **strict data minimization**: cooks receive the exact culinary instructions, timings, and dietary alerts needed to prepare food safely, without exposing members' personal phone numbers, emails, passwords, financial records, or detailed addresses.

## 4. Personal Data Minimization Verification

The digest JSON response was audited to confirm that zero sensitive attributes are transmitted:
- `user.phoneNumber`: Excluded
- `user.passwordHash`: Excluded
- `user.email`: Excluded
- `address.flatBuilding`: Excluded
- `payment`: Excluded

Cooks have read-only access to food preparation parameters. Domestic cooks cannot place orders, alter household budgets, invite or remove family members, or modify dietary restrictions.

---

## 5. Supervised Pilot Instructions for Households with Cooks

1. **Onboarding:** The household manager invites the cook using `role: 'COOK'` or generates the daily WhatsApp digest directly from their own dashboard.
2. **Review:** Household manager reviews the morning digest before sharing it with the cook.
3. **Emergency Changes:** If a guest arrives or a schedule shifts, the household manager updates the plan; the Maharaj digest updates immediately.
